Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Last Updated: October 8, 2026 • Developer: Ebram Wagdy • Status: Google Play Certified

1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to QuickNotes ("we", "our", or "the App"). QuickNotes is an offline-first, privacy-conscious personal productivity application built for capturing notes, organizing checklists, managing scheduled alarms, and transcribing thoughts.

Zero Mandatory Registration: QuickNotes does not require creating an account, email sign-up, or providing any personal identity data to use its core features.

2. Local-First Storage Architecture

Local On-Device All your notes, checklists, subtasks, color tags, audio recordings, and reminder dates are stored exclusively on your device inside an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database (quicknotes.db) and local application storage.

  • No Proprietary Cloud Servers: We do not run background servers to harvest, collect, sync, or sell your private notes.
  • Biometric Protection: If you lock notes with fingerprint or facial recognition, authentication is executed entirely on-device by Android’s hardware BiometricPrompt. No biometric data ever leaves your device's hardware security module.

3. Device Permissions & Actual Use Cases

QuickNotes requests only permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver user-initiated actions:

📷 Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)

Used strictly when you launch the document scanner to extract text from physical documents, whiteboards, or handwritten notes (OCR). Images are captured only upon explicit user action and are not sent to background surveillance servers.

🎙️ Microphone & Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Used to dictate notes via Android's built-in speech-to-text recognition engine and to record optional voice memos attached to your notes. Audio is recorded only while you actively hold or press the record button.

📍 Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Optional user-directed geotagging. If you choose to attach a location to a note, coordinates are queried once and saved locally in your database to render pins on the in-app interactive map. Location is never tracked in the background.

⏰ Exact Alarms & Full-Screen Intent (USE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)

Powers the time-critical reminder and Alarm Clock utility. Exact alarm APIs guarantee reminders ring at the exact minute even when the phone is in Doze/Idle power saving. Full-screen intent displays the incoming ringing screen over the lock screen so you can immediately Snooze or Dismiss active alarms.

4. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

External Processing (User-Initiated Only) QuickNotes includes optional intelligent productivity features:

Google Gemini Generative AI

If and only if you explicitly click to use AI features (such as "Summarize with AI", "Suggest Tags", "Smart Image OCR", or "Semantic Search"), the specific note content or image is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to the Google Gemini API for natural language processing.

  • Data transmitted is governed by Google’s Cloud and API Privacy Policies.
  • Your note contents are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • AI features are completely optional; all basic note-taking, checklists, alarms, and local searches function 100% offline without AI.

Map Tile Service

When viewing the interactive notes map, the map renders visual map tiles via public OpenStreetMap tile servers over secure HTTPS. No personal note data is sent to map servers.

6. Data Control, Retention, and Permanent Deletion

You retain full sovereign control over all data created in QuickNotes:

  • Delete Individual Items: You can delete or move any note or task to Trash at any moment.
  • "Delete All Notes": An in-app one-click option completely purges all notes, attachments, and subtasks from the local database.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling QuickNotes immediately and permanently purges all local databases, preferences, and caches from your device.
